Cookie Policy

Last updated: May 23, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains exactly what cookies and local storage the Library uses. We keep it minimal.

1. Cookies

gl_token (Authentication Cookie)

  • Type: Essential / strictly necessary
  • Purpose: Keeps you signed in to your account
  • Contents: An encrypted JWT token containing your account ID and email
  • Duration: 30 days
  • HttpOnly: Yes — this cookie cannot be read by JavaScript, protecting it from XSS attacks
  • Secure: Yes (in production) — only sent over HTTPS
  • SameSite: Lax — protects against CSRF attacks

This is the only cookie the Library sets. It is strictly necessary for the service to function when you are signed in.

2. localStorage

The Library uses your browser's localStorage to remember your preferences. This data stays on your device and is never sent to our servers.

Items Stored

  • gl-muted — whether you have muted the ambient sounds (true/false)
  • gl-volume — your preferred volume level (0 to 1)
  • gl-theme — your visual theme preference (dark/light)
  • gl-tomes — the number of ebooks you have generated (displayed as a counter)

You can clear these at any time by clearing your browser's site data for greatlibrary.ai.

3. Third-Party Cookies

The Library does not set third-party tracking cookies. We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, advertising networks, or any other third-party tracking technology that sets cookies in your browser.

If you sign in via Google One Tap, Google may set its own cookies as part of the authentication flow, governed by Google's privacy policy.

The LemonSqueezy payment overlay (loaded only when you initiate a payment) may set cookies as part of the checkout process, governed by LemonSqueezy's privacy policy.

4. Embedded Scripts

The Library loads the following external script:

  • LemonSqueezy checkout script (app.lemonsqueezy.com/js/lemon.js) — enables the in-page payment overlay. This script is loaded on the Library page but only activates when you click a payment button.
